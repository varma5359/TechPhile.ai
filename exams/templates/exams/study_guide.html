YOUR STUDY SESSION

A focused 4-step cycle

Keep each session small enough to finish and specific enough to learn from.

01

Set one clear goal

Pick one subject, level, and set. Before starting, write down what you want to understand or improve.

02

Try before checking

Answer from your own understanding first. For tricky questions, note why you chose an answer instead of guessing and moving on.

03

Review the reasoning

After the set, compare your answers with the explanations that are available. Identify whether a miss came from a concept gap, a misread, or a rushed choice.

04

Choose what comes next

Revisit the relevant lesson or topic, then try a related set later. Move up a level when the fundamentals feel steady.

FOR STUDENTS

Build understanding, not just a score

Use exam sets to check a subject after studying it. Keep a short list of concepts to revisit, and use the answer explanations to correct misunderstandings.

FOR JOB SEEKERS

Connect practice to the role

Choose a career path that matches your goal, work through its lessons and scenario questions, and explain your reasoning aloud as interview practice.

Make review actionable. End each session with one sentence: “Next, I’ll practice…” A small next step makes it easier to return.
