Job Market Evolution: Then vs. Now

The job market landscape has transformed dramatically. Understanding these changes is key to positioning yourself for success.

Past Job Market

  • More job openings available
  • Fewer candidates per position
  • Basic technical skills were sufficient
  • Straightforward interview processes
  • Minimal online application requirements

Current Job Market

  • Intense competition for fewer roles
  • Hundreds of applicants per opening
  • Demand for specialized, advanced skills
  • AI screening and automated processes
  • Multiple rigorous interview stages
